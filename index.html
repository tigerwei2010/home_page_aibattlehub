About Us

A start-up building for the people in the game.

AI Battle, Inc. is an independent start-up based in Seattle. We build TeamClash — team ratings, game highlights, and team chat for youth soccer.

We want to take the friction out of a season. The information already exists — scores, standings, rosters, video — but it is scattered across league sites, club portals, and a dozen group threads. The families actually living the season end up doing the most work to keep up with it. TeamClash puts it in one place: an honest rating, a highlight worth keeping, and somewhere for the team to talk.

And we want to back the people who hold youth soccer together. Coaches and club staff keep teams running, mostly on their own time. We are not going to charge them for the tools that make it easier — verified coaches get TeamClash Pro membership free, and so do the team staff they bring on.

Our users include minors, so we hold the product to a stricter standard than the law asks of us. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We decline registration for anyone under 13. We strip location and device metadata from every photo and video before it is stored. And our ratings are statistical estimates published for interest and conversation — never an official evaluation of any child.

People are the measure of all things. Even AI. It is on the front of this page because it is the test we apply to what we ship.
